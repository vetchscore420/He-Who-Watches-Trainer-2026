## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **He Who Watches**. This trainer provides convenience options for navigating the labyrinth and experimenting with its gravity-based puzzles. It supports movement, environmental interaction, arrow use, and recovery from failed platforming attempts without adding combat mechanics that are not present in the game.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to explore the labyrinth freely, practice gravity-shifting movement, experiment with puzzle solutions, locate secrets, or reduce frustration while solving the game's more than 100 handcrafted puzzles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Safe Movement** — Reduces the consequences of dangerous traversal and helps the player move through walls, ceilings, and narrow puzzle routes more safely.
- **Gravity Control** — Provides optional assistance while shifting between floors, walls, and ceilings during gravity-based puzzles.

### 📌 Puzzle

- **Unlimited Arrow Use** — Allows repeated use of the magical arrow for opening doors, moving blocks, activating switches, and using its chain to cross gaps.
- **Puzzle State Protection** — Helps preserve the current environmental setup while experimenting with blocks, switches, doors, and other interactive puzzle elements.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Fall Recovery** — Provides a quick recovery option after missed jumps, failed gravity transitions, or accidental falls.
- **Secret Area Assistance** — Makes exploration more convenient for players searching the labyrinth for hidden paths, cryptic symbols, and optional secrets.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — He Who Watches has no conventional combat system. The bow and magical arrow are puzzle-solving tools rather than weapons.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game first, load a save, and activate the trainer only after entering gameplay. Use movement assistance when crossing gaps or navigating surfaces affected by gravity. Disable the option before entering precise puzzle sequences if normal positioning is required.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional inventory system. Trainer options related to item use apply to the magical bow and its single magical arrow, which are used to manipulate doors, blocks, switches, and traversal points throughout the labyrinth.

### World & Environment

Explore carefully and use the trainer's gravity and recovery options to inspect unusual routes, hidden chambers, and environmental details. Secret paths and symbols are often found away from the most obvious solution, so controlled experimentation can make thorough exploration easier.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat gameplay in He Who Watches. The bow is an environmental interaction tool, not a combat weapon, and trainer options should be used for puzzle manipulation and movement rather than damage or enemy-related functions.
